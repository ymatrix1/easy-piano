# Piano samples

Acoustic grand piano notes from **FluidR3_GM**, as rendered to MP3 by
[gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts)
(MIT, see `LICENSE-midi-js-soundfonts.txt`; FluidR3_GM by Frank Wen is
distributed under the MIT license by that project).

One sample every 3 semitones from C2 to C7; the synth pitch-shifts the nearest
sample for the notes in between (`app/lib/audio/`).
